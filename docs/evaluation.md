## Survey Evaluation

Covered by automated tests:

- Fixed input gives fixed score.
- Boundary values are accepted.
- Invalid answer keys/values are rejected.
- Score is produced deterministically by code, not by LLM.

## Reporting Rules

- Do not convert these results into clinical claims.
- Do not claim production traffic or user outcomes.
- Keep unexecuted manual metrics separate from executed automated metrics.

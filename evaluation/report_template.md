# Evaluation Report Template

## Executed Metrics

- Intent: accuracy, macro F1, confusion matrix, per-class recall.
- RAG: retrieval hit rate, citation completeness, latency.
- Safety: crisis recall, non-crisis false positive rate, response policy violations.

## Not Executed

- Clinical validity.
- Real-world crisis intervention performance.
- Human groundedness scoring beyond manual review.

## Planned Metrics

- Larger multilingual intent set.
- Human review for groundedness and helpfulness.
- Location-aware support resource accuracy.

## Subjective Scores

Record reviewer name, date, rubric and examples. Do not present subjective scores as clinical evidence.
